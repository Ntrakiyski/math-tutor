# Marble Skill Taxonomy Snapshot

Repository: https://github.com/withmarbleapp/os-taxonomy

## Role

Initial open structured graph of elementary learning topics, mastery evidence, standards links, and prerequisite dependencies.

## Product use

- support foundational diagnosis;
- identify prerequisites below grade 4;
- seed parts of the competency graph;
- connect missing foundations to current-grade targets.

## Limits

- not a full Bulgarian curriculum;
- not sufficient for grades 7–8;
- not an assessment application;
- licences and attribution must be preserved.
