# DeepMind Mathematics Dataset Snapshot

Repository: https://github.com/google-deepmind/mathematics_dataset

## Role

Reference and starting point for deterministic question-generation patterns.

## Useful areas

- arithmetic;
- comparison;
- measurement;
- place value;
- factors and multiples;
- algebra;
- sequences;
- probability.

## Product decision

Adapt generator ideas to Bulgarian grade expectations, language, misconceptions, widgets, and teacher-reviewed difficulty. Do not use raw output as a finished child-learning experience.
