# Licensing Rules

## Before ingestion

Record source owner, title, edition, publication date, URL, licence, attribution, commercial-use permission, adaptation permission, translation permission, redistribution permission, and stated AI restrictions.

## Decisions

### Allowed for direct use

Only when the exact version permits intended commercial use and adaptation.

### Reference only

Material informs independent curriculum research but is not copied, translated, transformed, or embedded.

### Partnership required

Commercial textbooks, publisher tests, restricted platforms, or unclear rights.

### Original equivalent

Use official or copyrighted material to understand format and expectation, then create an independently authored task connected to the same mathematical skill.

## Separation

Keep open curriculum data, proprietary learner data, original product content, and third-party reference documents as clearly separated layers.
