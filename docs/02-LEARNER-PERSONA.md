# Learner Persona

## Definition

The Learner Persona is a custom, mathematics-specific, evidence-based personalization model. It is not a psychological profile or general biography.

It answers:

> How should this learner be taught this concept in this session?

## Persona categories

### Communication

- preferred language;
- vocabulary complexity;
- explanation length;
- amount of information per turn;
- typing versus buttons;
- text, voice, diagram, or action preference.

### Mathematical representations

- objects and counters;
- base-ten blocks;
- fraction bars and circles;
- number lines;
- diagrams;
- tables;
- symbolic notation;
- worked steps;
- practical contexts.

Representation preferences must be scoped to a domain or skill.

### Cognitive support

- optimal step size;
- effective hint depth;
- working-memory load;
- progressive reveal;
- response to worked examples;
- response to error correction;
- transition from concrete to symbolic.

### Motivation and behavior

- feedback that works;
- challenge tolerance;
- warm-up needs;
- boredom and frustration signals;
- retry behavior;
- guessing patterns;
- help-seeking behavior;
- average focused session length.

## Evidence progression

```text
Observation
→ Hypothesis
→ Stable strategy pattern
→ Operational persona guidance
```

One event must not become a permanent trait.

Prefer narrow claims:

> Number lines improve decimal-magnitude performance.

Avoid broad labels:

> The learner is visual.

## Confidence and provenance

Every inferred persona claim should contain scope, confidence, evidence count, supporting sessions, last confirmed date, and whether it was explicit or inferred.

## Corrections and decay

The learner or guardian should be able to understand and correct what the app believes. The system must support correction, deletion, supersession, conflict marking, confidence reduction, and expiration of weak patterns.
