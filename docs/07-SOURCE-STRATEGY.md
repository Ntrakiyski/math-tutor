# Source Strategy

## Purpose

The product needs Bulgarian alignment and a legally usable content base. These are separate requirements.

```text
Bulgarian official sources
→ define what must be learned and examined

Open and licensed educational sources
→ support reusable lesson and assessment design

Original content
→ becomes the main proprietary adaptive library
```

## Official Bulgarian sources

Use for grade expectations, terminology, exam structure, difficulty calibration, scoring, approved textbook metadata, and curriculum changes.

Key sources:

- Ministry curriculum pages for grades 4–8;
- grade 4 NVO;
- grade 7 NVO;
- mathematics olympiad and competition archives;
- approved textbook lists;
- Digital Backpack as a research platform.

Public availability does not automatically grant unrestricted commercial republication rights. Preserve references and create original equivalents unless rights are confirmed.

## Open educational sources

Potentially reusable after version-specific licence review:

- Marble Skill Taxonomy;
- Illustrative Mathematics first edition;
- eligible Open Up Resources materials;
- DeepMind Mathematics Dataset;
- selected original OpenStax editions;
- Manim Community Edition.

## Reference-only sources

- commercial Bulgarian textbooks;
- Digital Backpack content;
- Khan Academy;
- publisher test books;
- OSSU Math and linked third-party courses.

## Target content mix

```text
20% official Bulgarian alignment and calibration
20% openly licensed educational structures
60% original, generated, and teacher-reviewed content
```

## Review rule

No document, book, task bank, or dataset enters the product content pipeline without a recorded source and licence decision.
