# Bulgarian Grades 4–8 Curriculum Graph

## Goal

Build a structured mathematical competency graph aligned with the Bulgarian education system for grades 4–8.

The graph must support a learner who may be enrolled in a higher grade while missing foundations from earlier grades.

## Curriculum layers

### 1. Bulgarian expected outcomes

Official Bulgarian Ministry of Education programs define what is expected in each grade, official terminology, topic boundaries, competencies, and learning outcomes.

### 2. Foundational prerequisite graph

Marble Skill Taxonomy and reviewed extensions help decompose broad curriculum outcomes into smaller prerequisites.

### 3. Grade 4–8 bridge

The key product asset is an independently authored graph connecting:

```text
Foundational number sense
→ arithmetic
→ fractions and decimals
→ ratios and percentages
→ rational numbers
→ elementary algebra
→ geometry and measurement
→ grade 7 and grade 8 readiness
```

### 4. School and exam alignment

Connect competencies to grade, official outcome, NVO relevance, textbook sequence, assessment type, misconceptions, widgets, and Manim explanation templates.

## Competency record direction

Each skill should eventually include:

- stable identifier;
- Bulgarian name;
- plain-language description;
- expected grade;
- mathematical domain;
- hard and soft prerequisites;
- evidence of mastery;
- misconception patterns;
- diagnostic question families;
- teaching widget families;
- animation families;
- official source references;
- review status.

## Grade roles

- **Grade 4:** elementary arithmetic, place value, measurement, geometry, problem solving, and NVO readiness.
- **Grade 5:** bridge into deeper fractions, decimals, measurement, geometry, and structured problems.
- **Grade 6:** rational-number thinking, proportional relationships, geometry, algebraic preparation, and applied problems.
- **Grade 7:** rational numbers, algebraic reasoning, equations, geometry, modelling, and NVO preparation.
- **Grade 8:** more formal algebra, functions and graphs, geometry, reasoning, and high-school readiness.

## Remediation rule

Grade enrollment does not control the starting point. The system finds the lowest important missing prerequisite and constructs a dignified recovery path toward the current grade expectation.
